# ITEX / QS evidence roadmap — honest claims and next studies

## Positioning

**Human-Centred Responsible Generative AI Microcredentials with Adaptive Learning**

- **CulturaGenAI Academy:** multilingual ethical GenAI learning, CADG cultural suitability, learner reflection and six microcredentials.
- **m-DOAI AdaptEngine:** reusable explainable rule-based and provisional BKT adaptive practice engine.
- **NADI:** embodied visual companion; offline deterministic demo, optional server-hosted LLM with bounded read-only tools and explicit human oversight.

**Theoretical distinction:** CADG governs interface and cultural appropriateness; proposed m-DOAI guides human–AI learning activity; established BKT and rule-based algorithms support transparent adaptivity. These are integrated applications of established and proposed theories, not invented fundamental algorithms.

## Proposed research objectives (competition-safe)

1. To design a culturally appropriate, accessible learning environment for responsible GenAI microcredentials using CADG and proposed m-DOAI constructs.
2. To develop and demonstrate explainable, performance-informed adaptive learning support for progressive GenAI competency acquisition.
3. To integrate a supervised embodied learning companion with limited, transparent and accountable AI-assisted guidance.
4. To evaluate usability, ethical GenAI competency and implementation feasibility across learner groups and institutional settings.

Objective 4 is an **intended study**; no new intervention outcome is claimed by this software release.

## Proposed validation pathway

- **Round 1:** Expert review of course content, ethical safeguards, CADG applicability and m-DOAI assessment rubric; record a content-validity plan without misrepresenting provisional scales.
- **Round 2:** Accessibility/usability testing with meaningful sample of learner languages and needs, using authentic tasks and observed interaction problems.
- **Round 3:** Feasibility pilot: measure pre/post knowledge, source verification, AI-use disclosure, learner agency, task performance, time-on-task, teacher approval workload and ethical incidents.
- **Round 4:** Multi-institution deployment and independent analysis of transferability across contexts; monitor statistical uncertainty and domain/language differences.
- **Operational readiness:** penetration tests, backup restore tests, secure staff onboarding, model provider risk review, availability and performance monitoring, incident-response drills.

Prior m-DOAI coursework pilots can motivate design but **cannot be represented as new CulturaGenAI v3 performance results**.

## What NOT to claim

- Do not say "novel BKT algorithm," "autonomous AI certificates," "accredited by UPSI/MQA" or "TRL 9".
- Do not use dummy-data dashboard metrics as real participant evidence.
- Do not promise a particular ITEX medal or QS outcome.
- Do not imply that a polished poster or passed code tests establishes learning effectiveness or commercial scale.
