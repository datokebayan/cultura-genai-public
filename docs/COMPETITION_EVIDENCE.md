## Claims NOT yet supported

- Actual improvement in learner GenAI competencies, satisfaction or course-completion rates.
- Official accreditation, QR-verifiable credentials or recognition by government/professional bodies.
- Production-secure status, cross-institution isolation in live deployment, live AI safety or independent pentest.
- Benchmark capacity, demonstrated technical scalability, high availability or Technology Readiness Level 9.
- Real operational ROI, signed customers or ITEX/QS awards.

## Research framing

**Research gap:** UNESCO competencies, human-centred design and knowledge tracing are established strands; the locally contextual CADG-informed / proposed m-DOAI workflow connecting adaptive practice, learner evidence and educator review is an *implementation for empirical evaluation*, not a universally novel or validated theory.

**Meaningful educational outcome:** learners should be able to articulate GenAI limitations, protect private information, verify sources, explain their ethical decisions and demonstrate iterative human–AI practice, rather than solely passing a quiz.
