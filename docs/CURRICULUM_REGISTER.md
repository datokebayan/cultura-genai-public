## Assessment and credential policy

- Each module requires D/O/A/I learner evidence, sources and an ethical-use declaration, reviewed by an authorised educator.
- The secure pilot grades 18 final exam questions server-side, three per module; pass threshold 80%, maximum two graded submissions in the demonstration workflow.
- Only after the pass requirement and all six module evidence approvals can the authenticated learner request a pilot completion record.
- The public Academy badge, the completion record and the PowerPoint certificate design **must not be represented as accredited certificates**, unless approved by the issuing institution with verified identity and policies.
- BKT parameters and adaptation thresholds are provisional; they are not evidence that the learning gains are proven.
